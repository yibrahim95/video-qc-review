The biggest class in the audit was the caption landing on the B-roll's own text. It hit 7 of 11 videos before and produced most of the Criticals. It is now zero in 27.

The part that is not a zero. Making the gates honest surfaced defects the audit never counted, so they have no before number: 16 exposed jump cuts, 4 caption dropouts under a second, and 3 videos whose finals carry repeated frames from the caption compositor. Fourteen of the 27 are completely clean. The rest average about one finding each, against 5.7 before.

The three things you flagged

Change 7 needed Change 6's accuracy pass

Done. 36 videos and 1,216 frames judged blind. Clean videos passed 4 of 4 with no false alarms, bad videos failed 6 of 7, and the one miss was a severity call rather than a blind spot. It also caught a real collision the manual audit had cleared.

The framerate judder was still showing up

Traced. It is neither capture nor an export setting. Capture, prep and assembly all carry zero repeated frames; they appear at the caption compositing step, which re-renders frame by frame and occasionally returns the same frame twice. Two measurement bugs had been hiding it.

The summary line credited three fixes that were one

Corrected to four, with a visible dated note on the review page. There is now a single lookup table reconciling the change numbers, the audit's fix numbers and the gate numbers.

What the measurement found wrong with itself

Nine gates were green-lighting work they had not read.

Measuring live output surfaced that several checks were passing files they never actually read, which is the same failure this rock exists to close. A skipped check now reports as incomplete instead of silently passing, the framerate gate reads the beats the assembler actually cut, the caption gates no longer produce false failures on correct videos, and no single gate can crash the run and take the other videos with it. Every one was fixed before the numbers above were taken.

Open, and not blocking

  • Caption compositor. Render the caption layer with alpha and overlay it, so assembled frames pass through untouched. Removes the repeated frames.
  • Capture standard. Everything is 25fps end to end while phones present at 60Hz. Worth a deliberate call on moving to 30fps.
  • 16 exposed jump cuts and 4 caption dropouts. Both are now caught by gates that did not exist in July, so they are tracked work rather than surprises.
